PABLO evaluates a candidate task or project across five dimensions before
it goes on the backlog at all:

- **Purpose** — the actual reason for doing it.
- **Advantage** — the edge it provides over not doing it, or over an
  alternative.
- **Benefit** — the concrete positive outcomes it produces.
- **Longevity** — how long that impact lasts.
- **Outlay** — what it costs to do — time, money, complexity, risk.

**Why it matters:** Purpose and Benefit alone make almost anything sound
worth doing — it's Longevity and Outlay that surface the cases where the
cost outlasts the payoff, or the win is real but temporary. Naming all
five forces a comparison the sponsor can actually be held to, instead of
a one-line justification that only covers the flattering half.

**How to apply:** use PABLO earlier in the pipeline than [[moscow]] or
[[invest]] — it's for deciding whether a task or project earns a spot on
the backlog at all, before its stories are written and prioritized.
