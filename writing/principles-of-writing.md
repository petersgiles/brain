
# Cognitive Patterns - Frameworks of writing

## Multifacited Topic Elaboration

Topic followed by comment

Topic -> Comment
Topic -> Comment
Topic -> Comment
Topic -> Comment

in a paragraph the topic should be easy to identify. positionally. so maintain reference to topic consistently at or near the start of the sentence, commentary should follow.

> [BAD]
> Things that happen are not what produces  happiness. Good fortune and random chance  don't result in it either. Money can't  buy it and power can't command it. Outside events do not determine it. Our  interpretation of those events does.  Preparation, cultivation, and private defense  by each person are what this condition requires.

A better example

> [GOOD] 
> Happiness is not something that happens. It is  not the result of good fortune or random chance.It is not something that money can buy or power  command. It does not depend on outside events, but rather on how we interpret them. Happiness,  in fact, is a condition that must be prepared for, cultivated, and defended privately by each person.

This pattern keeps the reader focused on the mutli faceted examination of an idea, in this case happiness, it can created a stagnant dry feeling to the text, should be reserved for unpacking multiple ideas about a single thing.
